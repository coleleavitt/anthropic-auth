## What's fixed

- **Vault quota readings are no longer dropped.** In vault mode with several OpenCode or Pi processes, the log could fill with `Timed out acquiring claustrum-roster-write lock` and `Account usage publication unavailable`, each one a quota reading that wasn't saved, so routing and the sidebar could show stale quota. A served response held the vault roster lock while it waited to save its own bookkeeping, and every other process gave up after 5 seconds. Responses now save under their own lock only and recheck the account just before writing, so an account that changed meanwhile is refused rather than overwritten. (#431)
- **OpenCode 2 keeps the last quota reading on exit.** It now waits for pending quota readings to be saved before closing, as OpenCode 1 and Pi already did.

## Under the hood

- `@cortexkit/common-auth` 0.23.0: it retries shared vault quota writes instead of dropping them when another process holds the roster lock, and gives the sidebar its short quota labels (`Fa`).

To upgrade, quit OpenCode and run `npx @cortexkit/opencode-anthropic-auth@2.2.2 setup`, or change the version in your OpenCode config.
