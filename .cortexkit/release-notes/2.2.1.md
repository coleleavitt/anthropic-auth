## What's fixed

- **The expanded quota sidebar is readable again.** Since 2.0.3, at the sidebar's real width (36 columns on OpenCode 1 once the scrollbar shows, 37 on OpenCode 2), model rows used the full scope name (`Fable only`), which squeezed the bars, and each row's percent ran into its reset time (`100% left6d23h`) while pacing lines wrapped. Model rows read `Fa` again, as the collapsed line does. There's always a space before the reset; when a row doesn't fit, the reset is dropped before the percent is cut, and pacing lines stay on one line. This applies to OpenCode 1, OpenCode 2 and the `/claude` quota view.

To upgrade, quit OpenCode and run `npx @cortexkit/opencode-anthropic-auth@2.2.1 setup`, or change the version in your OpenCode config.
