## Fixes

- **Ultra accounts work again.** Anthropic renamed Max 20x to Ultra and removed the five-hour limit for verified Ultra accounts. The plugin treated the missing five-hour reading as unknown quota and, with the default fail-closed setting, refused those accounts even when their weekly limit had room. A reading that has the weekly limit but no five-hour limit now means the five-hour limit doesn't apply. The weekly and Fable limits still apply, and a missing or failed reading is still treated as unknown. (#572)
- **Clearer refusal when your own limits block every vault account.** In vault mode, a request refused because your quota floors, killswitch limits or a model's weekly limit exclude every account now says which limit blocked it and where to change it (`/claude` → Killswitch / Quota), instead of asking you to sign in again. A real sign-in problem keeps its message. (#567)

To upgrade, quit OpenCode and Pi and run `npx @cortexkit/opencode-anthropic-auth setup`, or change the version in your OpenCode config.
