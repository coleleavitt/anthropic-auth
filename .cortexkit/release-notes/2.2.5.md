## What's fixed

- **OpenCode 2 retries use the right account.** When a request failed and OpenCode retried it, the plugin could be asked to choose an account more than once for the same attempt. A pending retry could then be used up and the retry refused, or a conversation's account pin could be deleted or moved to an account that wasn't used. The plugin now chooses the retry's account once and keeps it, and an account it chose but OpenCode didn't use is released.

## Documentation

- The docs now say what their paths and shell examples mean on Windows (`%USERPROFILE%`, `%TEMP%`, PowerShell), with one shared note in the environment guide.

## Under the hood

- Built on `@cortexkit/common-auth` 0.25.0.

To upgrade, quit OpenCode and Pi and run `npx @cortexkit/opencode-anthropic-auth setup`, or change the version in your OpenCode config.
